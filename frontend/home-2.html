The timeline

A year in the life of an applicant

The admissions calendar is long, but predictable. We break it into phases so nothing sneaks up on you.

Phase 1 - Explore

Clarify goals, strengths and possible fields of study.

Phase 2 - Build

Strengthen your profile and draft a balanced university list.

Phase 3 - Write

Essay brainstorming, multiple drafts and counselor feedback.

Phase 4 - Apply

Submit early action and regular decision applications on time.

Phase 5 - Decide

Compare offers, financial aid and fit before you commit.

Mentoring approach

Guidance, not gatekeeping

Our counselors explain the "why" behind every recommendation, so students learn to make their own decisions long after the application is submitted.

Open communicationRegular check-ins, clear progress updates and honest feedback keep students and families informed.
Goal-drivenEach meeting focuses on a practical milestone, with next steps and deadlines matched to the student’s application plan.
Ethical guidanceWe give realistic advice, protect each student’s authentic story and never promise admission outcomes.
Student-firstRecommendations consider interests, wellbeing, budget and long-term goals—not pressure or rankings alone.
