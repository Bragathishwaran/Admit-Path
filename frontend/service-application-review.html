Application Review

What Application Review covers

Submitting is the end of a long checklist. Before you press the button, we read the whole file the way an admissions officer does: activities, resumes, forms and essays, checking for inconsistency, missing detail and anything that could raise a question you are not expecting.

Reviews are returned as a prioritised list, so you know what to fix first. We mark the items that block submission separately from the items that simply make the application stronger, and we do not touch a single word without explaining why.

Book the review at least two weeks before your earliest deadline, so there is still time to act on the feedback.
