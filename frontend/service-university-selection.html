University Selection

What University Selection covers

A good list is not the longest list. We research programs, campus culture, cost and outcomes so that your final list is balanced between ambition and realism, and so that every entry on it has a reason you can defend in an interview.

Shortlisting is also a budgeting exercise. Two schools with the same sticker price can offer very different net costs once aid, housing and commute are taken into account, and we make those comparisons explicit rather than leaving them to the aid calculator at midnight in October.

Shortlisting is worth doing before applications open, so the list can still be adjusted when a counselor suggests a new option.
